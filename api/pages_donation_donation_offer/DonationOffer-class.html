DonationOffer

DonationOffer class

What the donation page offers, as the news feed declares it.

Donorbox charges in the campaign's own currency, so a non-Euro campaign is a change Open Food Facts makes on both sides at once: the feed says what the app shows, the campaign says what the donor is charged.
