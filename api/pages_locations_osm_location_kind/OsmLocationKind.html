OsmLocationKind

OsmLocationKind enum

What an OsmLocation is, from its OSM tag (OsmLocation.osmKey and OsmLocation.osmValue), e.g. shop=supermarket.

OsmLocation.osmType cannot be used: it is the kind of OSM element (node, way or relation), not the kind of place.
