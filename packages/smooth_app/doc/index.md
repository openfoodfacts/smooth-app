## About the App

Open Food Facts is a collaborative, free and open database of food products from around the world.
The mobile application (built with Flutter) allows users to:

- Scan food barcodes to view Nutri-Score, NOVA group, Eco-Score, and ingredient details.
- Compare products and search for healthier or more eco-friendly alternatives.
- Contribute missing products, nutrition tables, and packaging information by uploading photos.
